How a game gets processed. 1 · Copy the video into the clips folder and press Add a game. 2 · Mark the court (Mark court on the row: about ten minutes of clicking). 3 · Press Run on Colab: the video and the court files go to your Google Drive. 4 · Open Colab and press Run all there; it works through every game in the inbox and the results come back here by themselves. Stop the Colab runtime when its log says "Nothing to do".

Add a game

  1. Copy the game video into the clips folder: ... (paste that into the address bar of Windows Explorer).
  2. Press and the video appears in the list below.
  3. Choose it, name the opponent, say home or away and the date, and save. The final score can be typed now or after the game is watched.
